    角色故事

    若有介绍文本被隐藏，请上下滑动区域以查看完整内容。

    People still speak with wistful affection of the mime who burned so brightly and disappeared so soon. They remember her melancholy white-painted face, both comic and sorrowful, and the immeasurable feeling she poured into that elusive, silent world.
    Night after night onstage, Cléo Decroux stood beneath the harsh white glare of the lights, panic plain upon her face. Her throat tightened. Her hands pressed and struck against the invisible glass wall before her, as though sheer force might shatter it.
    What almost no one knew was that, in Cléo's mind, it was never an act. The glass box around her was real. The audience's noise, cheerful yet indifferent, seemed to press against it from every side, driving it inward with relentless force. As the walls closed in, the air inside grew thinner and thinner.
    Even in sorrow, she still remembered how to shape invisible butterflies with her fingers. Once, they had come fluttering from a place that seemed to exist just alongside the real world, gentle creatures that soothed the hollow places in her heart and in the hearts of certain children.
    Cléo grew up in a tiny grocery shop in France. As a child, she refused to speak. Words always seemed less truthful to her than movement, so she chose instead to express herself through gesture. Her strange and uncontrollable gift did little to ease her parents' worries about the family business. At times, it inspired wonder, and at others, trouble. Even so, they did what they could to protect her. They allowed her to spend her days in an open lot near the crowded market streets, and there Cléo gathered around her a little band of companions, children who had met misery too early in life: children trapped in desperate poverty, children said to suffer from some "odd illness," little beggars with nowhere else to go.
    Those were happy days, truly. Cléo Decroux, odd, impractical, and kindhearted, became their strange elder sister. With exaggerated gestures and astonishing whistles, she opened the gates to a world of her own making. At her fingertips lay a valley alive with butterflies. A broken crate became a tower filled with hidden treasure. Between bricks and stones, rivers of burning lava ran. The children threw themselves into these adventures with solemn delight. They searched, leaped, ran, and stared in wonder. Loneliness fell away. Hearts shriveled by real suffering slowly opened again, warmed by a sense of abundance that existed nowhere else and yet somehow felt entirely real.
    Imagination is a marvelous power, especially in the hands of someone who measures the world with her heart. But children grow up. In time, they all drifted into the adult world, as naturally and inevitably as water running downhill. Cléo was left alone in the empty lot, performing her little marvels for no one. What had once enchanted her playmates now earned only blank stares, wary looks, and open contempt. And when that cruelty finally reached her mother and father in ways too real to ignore, Cléo slipped away from home one night without a word.
    Later, she was discovered by a seasoned actor from an arts school. But the stage, and the distant audiences watching from the dark, were never a cure for her. After one performance, loneliness and exhaustion at last swallowed her whole. Cléo resolved to escape the glass box once and for all. More than anything, she longed to find the companions she had lost.

    即兴默剧
    数量：3
    冷却时间：5秒
    根据内心所涌现的不同情感，默剧艺人会表演两种不同的默剧。

    欣快的剧目
    默剧艺人撑开道具雨伞，欣快地模拟大风天舞伞的情境。在拟真的大风中，道具雨伞将牵引默剧艺人沿直线移动10.1米，撞到障碍物会提前停下，并在默剧艺人的身后创造出拟真墙体和拟真木板。当移动路径左右的空间不够时，拟真墙体无法生成；当默剧艺人停下位置附近没有固定的障碍物或存在可交互的场景组件时，拟真木板无法生成。
    当监管者25米内没有除默剧艺人外的其他求生者时，破坏拟真木板的速度降低15%。
    拟真木板被破坏的10秒后，拟真墙体消失；若未生成拟真木板，则拟真墙体会在生成的10秒后消失。
    忧虑的剧目
    默剧艺人将道具雨伞掷出，在忧虑中模拟重返舞台的情境。以道具雨伞为中心，创造一个圆形的拟真舞台，半径12.7米，持续15秒。当求生者进入拟真舞台后，道具雨伞会出现在离舞台中心最近的求生者位置，并产生一个半径4.2米的预演区域，道具雨伞将根据预演区域内的目标做出不同行为：
    如果预演区域内存在监管者，道具雨伞会优先攻击监管者；
    如果预演区域内存在求生者，道具雨伞会将其吸入，并在2秒后使其沿指定方向弹出一段距离，求生者也可以提前进行弹出。此后道具雨伞不再出现。

    道具雨伞出现的冷却时间为4秒。
    情感累积
    默剧艺人会收集不同行为的情感碎片，当欣快和忧虑的情感都充满时，默剧艺人才能使用手持物。默剧艺人可以自由切换表演的剧目，切换后两种情感的比例会发生变化。

    当默剧艺人移动、交互时，会随时间收集欣快的情感碎片。当默剧艺人使用木板砸中监管者时，欣快的情感将直接充满。

    即兴默剧 

    忧愁的剧目
    • 飞行时无法转向
    • 在范围内时可以连续多次控制监管者
    • 正常情况下伞不会自动追踪牵气球时的监管者

    在自身和此时的监管同处时触发雨伞后，自己远离雨伞判定范围，雨伞会控制监管者

    • 可以隔墙触发
    • 先判定求生者再判定屠夫

    残血的自己＞残血的求生者＞监管者＞满血的自己＞满血的求生者

    在同一范围内时

    监管者＞求生者

    • 求生者交互动作或被监管者控制时可以打断并带走
    • 起飞垂直高度为2层楼（平地上鬼屋），但是不能跨越月亮河
    • 起飞后可以打下,飞行时可以造成伤害
    • 若从高处跳下时被选中，伞的出发位置会改变成同位置y轴最低处开始飞行

    低处选中高处，伞会在高处求生者位置起飞 

    欣快的剧目
    • 在站台，鬼屋等路径较窄的区域无法生成墙体
    • 无法在已生成的墙体上再次生成板子和墙体
    • 若生成过程中被打断，则不会生成墙体
    道具雨伞

    可以更换道具
